## 🧭 Principles

```
01 — A BETTER FUTURE IS ACCESSIBLE
     Every tool we build is designed for as many people as possible,
     regardless of their device, location, or ability.
     Accessibility is not an afterthought — it's the foundation.

02 — THE BEST IS PRO
     Our best tools are built for professionals — the people who rely
     on them to do their best work. We prioritize depth, precision,
     and reliability over gimmicks and flash.

03 — IT SHOULD JUST WORK
     Every Filedocr tool is designed to be intuitive and reliable
     from the moment you first use it.
     We sweat the details so you don't have to.

04 — IT'S AVAILABLE TO EVERYONE
     We always provide a free tier that's genuinely useful, because
     everyone deserves access to good tools — not just those who can pay.
```
